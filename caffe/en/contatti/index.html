How to get here

  • By metro: Line 1, Toledo or Dante stations, a few minutes' walk away.
  • On foot: we are on via Toledo, between piazza Dante and piazza del Plebiscito, at the entrance to the Pignasecca market and the Quartieri Spagnoli.
  • By car: much of the city centre is a limited traffic zone (ZTL); it is best to leave your car in a car park and continue on foot or by metro.

Frequently asked questions

What are the opening hours?

We are open every day, Sundays included, from 6:30 am to 8:30 pm.

Where is the café?

At Piazza Carità 14 in central Naples, on Via Toledo near the Pignasecca market. Toledo and Dante stations on Metro Line 1 are a few minutes' walk away.

Can I see the menu online?

Yes: see the full menu with prices, or the interactive menu, available in English with an allergen filter.

Do you make your own pastries?

Yes. Our pastries are made in-house from family recipes handed down through the generations.

What can I have for breakfast?

A classic Italian breakfast with croissants, brioche, kranz and pain au chocolat, plus Neapolitan pastries such as sfogliatella, graffa and babà.

Do you serve an English breakfast?

Yes, every morning: eggs, bacon, sausages, beans, mushrooms, tomatoes, toast and tea.

How can I contact you?

Call +39 081 552 3488, or mobile +39 328 438 8404 (also WhatsApp), or find us on Instagram @ceraldicaffe.
