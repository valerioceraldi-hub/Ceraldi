Ceraldi Caffè / Pastry shop

The pastry shop: made in-house with family recipes

Everything in our display case is made in our own kitchens, with family recipes kept for over a hundred years.

All pastries on the menuGet directions

An old Neapolitan saying goes: "Naples has three beautiful things: the sun, the sea and the sfogliatella." It still holds true here. Ceraldi Caffè's pastries are made in-house: they are born in our kitchens every day, with family recipes kept for over a hundred years.

Neapolitan classics

  • Sfogliatella riccia and frolla, filled with ricotta, semolina, candied peel and cinnamon. And the Santa Rosa, with custard and black cherries.
  • Babà al rum, plain or with custard and black cherries, cream and strawberries, or chocolate.
  • Pastiera napoletana, with wheat berries, ricotta and orange blossom.
  • Zeppola di San Giuseppe, with custard and a black cherry.
  • Codina di aragosta, filled with chantilly cream, chocolate or pistachio.
  • Caprese cake with chocolate or lemon, delizia al limone, biscotto all'amarena, palmiers, francesine and mini pastries.

House creations

  • Cestino: the crisp layers of the sfogliatella shaped into a basket, filled with chantilly cream and berries.
  • Segreto amalfitano: limoncello babà with a heart of Amalfi lemon chantilly.
  • Babàmisù: a babà dome with mascarpone cream and coffee.
  • Pellecchiella: a tart made with Pellecchiella apricots from Vesuvius.
  • Apple cake with Annurca PGI apples, ricotta and pear, wild strawberry tart, custard and black cherry millefeuille.

Festive pastries

You can read the calendar in our display case: zeppole on 19 March, pastiera at Easter and the Neapolitan Christmas sweets in December.
