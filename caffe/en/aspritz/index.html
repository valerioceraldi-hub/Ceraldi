Ceraldi Caffè / Aspritz and aperitivo

Aspritz: our homemade Neapolitan spritz

It is six in the evening, the city lights come on and aperitivo time begins. The star is our own: the Aspritz.

Cocktails and drinksGet directions

Aspritz is our homemade aperitivo, created to celebrate the local area. It starts with a bitter we make ourselves, with Amalfi Coast citrus, herbs and roots that give it a young, fresh character. We finish it with sparkling Asprinio di Aversa, one of the oldest and most distinctive wines of Campania.

The result is a truly local spritz: fresh, citrusy and not too strong. A Neapolitan spritz from start to finish.

Other spritzes

Alongside the Aspritz: the classic Campari Spritz, Limoncello Spritz, Hugo with lime, mint and elderflower, Passion Spritz, Cynar Spritz, the Venetian spritz and two spritzes that speak Neapolitan, the Pignasecca Spritz (Aperol, Campari, Cynar and prosecco) and the Maradona Spritz, blue like curaçao.

Negroni, gin and tonic and wine

  • Negroni corner: classic, sour, oriental and the Tyson Negroni.
  • Gin and tonic corner, with selected gins and matching tonics.
  • Wines by the glass, including Campanian labels such as Fiano di Avellino DOCG.
  • Beers, including Neapolitan N'Artigiana on tap, and alcohol-free cocktails.

All served at your table in Piazza Carità, while the city slows down.
