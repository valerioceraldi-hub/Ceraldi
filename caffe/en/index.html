Craftspeople by passion, since 1973

The Ceraldi family's story in Naples cafés began in the early 1970s in Piazza Garibaldi. In 2002 we moved to Piazza Carità, on Via Toledo, steps from the Pignasecca market and the Spanish Quarter. Locals come in early for their first espresso and a warm sfogliatella, stop by during the day for something from the pastry case, and come back in the evening for aperitivo.

Our pastries are made in-house from family recipes kept for over a hundred years, alongside our own creations. It is an elegant Neapolitan bar, with mirrors, red leather armchairs and a busy counter.

Frequently asked questions

What are the opening hours?

We are open every day, Sundays included, from 6:30 am to 8:30 pm.

Where is the café?

At Piazza Carità 14 in central Naples, on Via Toledo near the Pignasecca market. Toledo and Dante stations on Metro Line 1 are a few minutes' walk away.

Can I see the menu online?

Yes: see the full menu with prices, or the interactive menu, available in English with an allergen filter.

Do you make your own pastries?

Yes. Our pastries are made in-house from family recipes kept for over a hundred years.

What can I have for breakfast?

A classic Italian breakfast with croissants, brioche, kranz and pain au chocolat, plus Neapolitan pastries such as sfogliatella, graffa and babà.

Do you serve an English breakfast?

Yes, every morning: eggs, bacon, sausages, beans, mushrooms, tomatoes, toast and tea.

How can I contact you?
