Our story

Three dates, one family

  1. 1973Piazza Garibaldi

    Michele Ceraldi opens his first café in front of the railway station, among travellers coming and going.

  2. 2002Piazza Carità

    We move to number 14, on Via Toledo: an empty corner that seemed to be waiting for us.

  3. TodayFather and sons

    Michele runs the café with Vincenzo and Valerio, from the pastry kitchen to the counter.
