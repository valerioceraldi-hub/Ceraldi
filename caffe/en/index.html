Craftspeople by passion, since 1973

For more than fifty years Ceraldi Caffè has been the café of Piazza Carità, between Via Toledo and the Pignasecca market. Locals come in early for their first espresso and a warm sfogliatella, stop by during the day for something from the pastry counter, and come back in the evening for aperitivo.

It is an elegant Neapolitan bar: mirrors, red leather seats, a busy counter and a pastry display refilled every day, following the city's tradition with a few house creations you won't find anywhere else.

In Naples coffee is never just coffee: it is the moment the city stops, even for a minute.

House specialities

Besides espresso and the classics of Neapolitan pastry, our counter serves coffees and cakes created at Ceraldi.

Frequently asked questions

What are the opening hours?

We are open every day, Sundays included, from 6:30 am to 8:30 pm.

Where is the café?

At Piazza Carità 14 in central Naples, between Via Toledo and the Pignasecca market. Toledo and Dante stations on Metro Line 1 are a few minutes' walk away.

Can I see the menu online?

Yes, the full menu with coffee, pastries, cocktails and allergens is online at ceraldicaffe.qromo.it.

What can I eat there?

Breakfast with Neapolitan pastries (sfogliatella, babà, pastiera, zeppole), cakes, a cold buffet and, in the evening, aperitivo with cocktails, beer and spirits.
